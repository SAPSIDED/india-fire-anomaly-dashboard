# Design Directions — India Fire Anomaly Intelligence Dashboard

## Three possible approaches

### 1. Sentinel Cartography
**Very Brief Intro:** A dark field-intelligence console inspired by geological survey instruments and contemporary civic data rooms. It treats the map as a living investigative surface rather than a decorative background.

**Probability:** 0.07

### 2. Monsoon Fieldbook
**Very Brief Intro:** A warm editorial research notebook using paper textures, map annotations, and monsoon-grey ink. It would make a complex technical topic feel measured, human, and academic.

**Probability:** 0.03

### 3. Signal Lattice
**Very Brief Intro:** A high-contrast systems interface built from translucent graph-paper panels, operational rails, and precise data glyphs. It emphasizes inference, uncertainty, and networked evidence.

**Probability:** 0.09

---

## Chosen Approach — Sentinel Cartography

### Design Movement
**Contemporary scientific instrumentation fused with editorial cartography.** The dashboard draws from control rooms, meteorological field tools, and high-end atlas layouts rather than consumer SaaS cards.

### Core Principles
1. **Evidence before alarm:** every visual cue points to the chain of validation, not merely a red heat dot.
2. **Asymmetric observability:** the map holds the primary field of attention while narrow diagnostic rails surface the decision context.
3. **Layered uncertainty:** transparency, blur, hairline contours, and muted baselines make confidence and ambiguity legible.
4. **Material restraint:** restrained glass panels, graphite surfaces, and only two high-salience alert colors prevent a cyberpunk appearance.

### Color Philosophy
The base is deep mineral graphite and blue-black, echoing nighttime satellite interpretation and preventing the map from becoming visually flat. A cooled turquoise represents verified data and infrastructure; **Signal Ember (#FF5A36)** is the ownable hazard color reserved for candidate industrial fires and high-risk evidence. Warm sand is used sparingly for explanatory copy so operational text never appears sterile.

### Layout Paradigm
The site is a **cartographic workbench**, not a centered landing page. A shallow command header anchors the page; the main map occupies an uneven left field, while an observational rail occupies the right. Below, the research evidence unfolds as a sequence of stitched lab plates: a six-layer validation ribbon, statistical models, data QA gates, and an implementation checklist.

### Signature Elements
1. **Thermal contours:** subtle concentric contour lines behind active detections and research metrics.
2. **Evidence traces:** a thin segmented path that shows a hotspot passing from raw detection to decision.
3. **Coordinate labels:** monospaced geospatial readouts and latitude-longitude ticks embedded in map panels.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions are investigative. Hovering a hotspot reveals only the essential satellite and location evidence; selecting it opens a deliberate verification sequence that visually evaluates each condition and produces a clearly caveated decision. Every interactive device answers “what evidence supports this?” instead of merely decorating the screen.

### Animation
The contour field drifts at an almost imperceptible rate. On hover, a hotspot gains a slow pulse and the evidence trace brightens. Selecting a detection advances validation gates with a 180–260 ms stagger and opens the decision dialog with opacity plus a small upward translation. Motion respects reduced-motion preferences and never suggests false real-time certainty.

### Typography System
**DM Mono** carries coordinates, timestamps, conditions, scores, and all data labels. **Manrope** carries navigation and body information; its rounded but technical shapes counterbalance the precise grid. Headlines use Manrope at heavy weight with a slight negative tracking, while research section titles use controlled sentence case rather than all caps.

### Brand Essence
**India Fire Anomaly Intelligence is an evidence-first thermal anomaly workbench for teams separating industrial fire hazards from routine heat sources.**

Personality: **forensic, composed, legible.**

### Brand Voice
Headlines are decisive but never overclaim; CTAs invite inspection rather than promise certainty. Microcopy describes what is observed, what is inferred, and what remains uncertain.

Example lines: “A hot pixel is evidence — not yet an incident.”

Example lines: “Verify the signal against place, time, and process.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The logo is a compact **broken contour ring** enclosing a small thermal core: a recognizable symbol for detecting an outlying heat signature within a mapped system. The wordmark pairs the mark with a tightly tracked, all-caps DM Mono label; the symbol can stand alone as a favicon.

### Signature Brand Color
**Signal Ember — #FF5A36.**

## Style Decisions

- The first viewport prioritizes the active map field and diagnostic rail; headline copy acts as a compact research framing device rather than a landing-page hero.
- Every major research section carries a recurring Sentinel Cartography primitive: segmented evidence trace, contour ring, validation gate, coordinate tick, or mono spatial readout.
- India specificity appears through regional latitude/longitude bounds, corridor notation, monsoon context, and administrative-map language—not decorative national imagery.
