# Land-Cover Evidence Research

ESA WorldCover is an appropriate public baseline dataset because the official project describes its products as free of charge and unrestricted for use under CC BY 4.0. The 2021 WorldCover v200 product is a global 10 m map based on Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 data with 11 land-cover classes. The planned evidence field will map the returned native class into a conservative plain-English category, preserving the provider attribution and not using it for Stage 1 classification.
